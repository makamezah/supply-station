How does a Supply Station make money?

The presentation below explains the cost of establishing a single solar-powered hub, how it generates revenue, and how long it takes to pay for itself.

1
The Money Coming In

Hubs make money in two simple ways: Families pay a small yearly membership fee (GH₵ 300), and we take a strict 20% margin on the food they buy.

Because the families pre-order and pre-pay, we carry zero debt for buying the food.

Social & Economic Wealth Protection

Aggregate Community Savings (Yearly)
GH₵ 21.6 Million

By bypassing the up to 500% informal market markup (especially during off-seasons), we give families a massive, systemic discount. This is money injected directly back into local communities.

How we calculate this:
  • Open Market Reality: A typical GH₵ 1,500/mo basket during seasonal spikes carries a 500% markup. True farm value = GH₵ 250.
  • Our Price: We take that GH₵ 250 cost + strict 20% margin = GH₵ 300/mo.
  • Family Savings: They save GH₵ 1,200/mo = GH₵ 14,400 per year.
  • Total Impact: GH₵ 14,400 × 1,500 families = GH₵ 21.6 Million.
