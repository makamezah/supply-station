The Mechanics of our Cost-Driven Model

Because our inventory is entirely customer-funded (prepaid through our digital platform), our hub carries zero debt for buying food. The only thing we need to pay off is the physical container and solar setup.

1. Our Hub Revenue Generation
Upfront Subscription: 100 families × GH₵ 300 = GH₵ 30,000
Total Food Sales (GMV): 100 families × GH₵ 1,500 × 12 = GH₵ 1,800,000
Gross Retail Margin (20%): GH₵ 360,000

2. The Cost-to-Revenue Ratio
You want to know how much it costs us to make a dollar. With an OpEx of GH₵ 60,000 and Revenue of GH₵ 390,000, the ratio is an incredibly lean 15.4%.

3. The Verdict
Once our hub hits its target capacity, it completely pays off its initial GH₵ 90,000 investment in just under 3.3 Months. Even if it takes us 3 months to onboard families, the hub pays for itself within its first 6 months of existence.
